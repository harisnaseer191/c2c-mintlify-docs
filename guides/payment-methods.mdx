## Overview

The `walletType` of an order names one **payment method**: a provider (JazzCash or Easypaisa) and what the customer pays to (an account number, a Till ID or a QR code). A deposit is only ever given a receiving account of exactly the method you requested.

## Deposit methods

| `walletType` | Method | The customer pays to | The customer enters on the payment page, before paying |
|--------------|--------|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| `JazzCash` | JazzCash account number | A JazzCash account number | Their own JazzCash account number (`03XXXXXXXXX`) |
| `JazzCashTillID` | JazzCash Till ID | A JazzCash Till ID | Their own JazzCash account number |
| `JazzCashQR` | JazzCash QR | The member's JazzCash QR code, which they scan | Their own JazzCash account number |
| `Easypaisa` | Easypaisa account number | An Easypaisa account number | Their own Easypaisa account number (`03XXXXXXXXX`) |
| `EasypaisaTillID` | Easypaisa Till ID | An Easypaisa Till ID | Their own Easypaisa account number |
| `EasypaisaQR` | Easypaisa QR | The member's Easypaisa QR code, which they scan | Their own Easypaisa account number |

No method asks for a transaction ID (TID) or IBAN digits any more. See [What the customer submits](#what-the-customer-submits).

`JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR` and `EasypaisaQR` are new values. `EasypaisaTillID` is the **Easypaisa** Till ID method.

<Note>
  `BankAccount` is still a valid `walletType`: it is used for [withdrawals](#withdrawals), and a deposit can be created with it. Bank accounts aren't offered on the hosted payment page for now, though. The payment link of a `BankAccount` deposit opens on the list of available methods, and the customer pays with one of the six methods above.
</Note>

## Exact matching

A deposit requested on a `walletType` is assigned only to a member who has a receiving account of **exactly that method**. No method falls back to another one, and never to another provider.

<Warning>
  **Changed behaviour.** `JazzCash` and `Easypaisa` used to cover the provider's Till IDs as well. They now mean the account number only.
</Warning>

| You request | Before | Now |
|-------------|--------|-----|
| `JazzCash` | A JazzCash account number **or** a JazzCash Till ID | A JazzCash account number only |
| `Easypaisa` | An Easypaisa account number **or** an Easypaisa Till ID | An Easypaisa account number only |
| `EasypaisaTillID` (was `TillID`) | An Easypaisa Till ID | Unchanged, under its new name |
| `JazzCashTillID` | Not available | A JazzCash Till ID only |
| `JazzCashQR` | Not available | A JazzCash QR code only |
| `EasypaisaQR` | Not available | An Easypaisa QR code only |

### If you already integrate

- The request and response fields are unchanged, and `JazzCash`, `Easypaisa` and `BankAccount` are requested as before.
- **The Easypaisa Till ID method is renamed from `TillID` to `EasypaisaTillID`.** `TillID` is no longer a valid `walletType`: a request that sends it is refused with `400`, like any unknown value.
- If you relied on `JazzCash` reaching JazzCash Till IDs, request `JazzCashTillID` for those payments.
- If you relied on `Easypaisa` reaching Easypaisa Till IDs, request `EasypaisaTillID` for those payments.
- To let customers pay by QR code, request `JazzCashQR` or `EasypaisaQR`.
- If your code validates `walletType` against a fixed list, add `JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR` and `EasypaisaQR`.

### When no account of the method is available

Creating the order never depends on a member being available: a deposit is always accepted as `Queued`, and a member is looked for only when the customer submits the account they pay from. C2C then looks for a member with a receiving account of the requested method and doesn't substitute another method. If none is free, the payment page shows **Assigning Collection Account** with a 10-second countdown and keeps looking. If no member becomes available within those 10 seconds, the order ends `CancelledTimeout` and the page shows "Failed to assign the order to a member. Please check with support." See the [Deposit flow](/guides/deposit-flow).

### When the customer changes the method

The payment page opens directly on the `walletType` you requested, with no method list. A customer only chooses a method themselves when they reach the page through a link that names none: the page then lists the methods that are enabled for your account and whose [amount limit](#amount-limits) covers the order (so it can show fewer than six), and until they submit their account they can go back with **Pay another way**. An order paid on another method than the one you requested asks for the account of that method's provider, and your fee is re-quoted for it. Once an account number, Till ID or QR code has been shown, the order stays on that method.

## What the customer submits

On all six methods the customer submits one thing on the [hosted payment page](/guides/hosted-payment-page), **before** they are shown where to pay: the **wallet account number they will pay from**.

| Methods | The customer enters |
|---------|---------------------|
| `JazzCash`, `JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR` | Their JazzCash account number: 11 digits starting with `03`, for example `03001234567` |
| `Easypaisa`, `EasypaisaTillID`, `EasypaisaQR` | Their Easypaisa account number: 11 digits starting with `03`, for example `03001234567` |

Only after they submit it does C2C assign a member and show that member's account number, Till ID or QR code. The customer then pays the exact amount in their wallet app. **Nothing is submitted after paying**: the assigned member confirms the order when the payment arrives from the account the customer gave.

<Warning>
  **Changed behaviour.** The account number and Till ID methods used to ask for the last digits of the transaction ID (TID) after paying, `EasypaisaQR` asked for the last 4 digits of the customer's IBAN, and the QR methods had an **I've Completed Payment** button. None of these exist any more. `txnRefDigitsRequired` is still accepted on [Create deposit](/api-reference/endpoints/create-deposit), but it is deprecated and no longer used.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The account the customer enters is their claim, not proof. The order completes only when the member confirms that the money arrived. The account a customer pays from isn't returned by the Merchant API or sent in webhooks.
</Note>

## Amount limits

Every order must be for an amount within the limit of its payment method. A limit applies to one order, and both ends are included: a `JazzCash` deposit of exactly `1000` or exactly `50000` is accepted.

### Deposits (pay-in)

| `walletType` | Minimum (PKR) | Maximum (PKR) |
|--------------|---------------|---------------|
| `Easypaisa` | 1,000 | 50,000 |
| `EasypaisaTillID` | 500 | 50,000 |
| `EasypaisaQR` | 500 | 50,000 |
| `JazzCash` | 1,000 | 50,000 |
| `JazzCashTillID` | 500 | 50,000 |
| `JazzCashQR` | 500 | 50,000 |

A `BankAccount` deposit is paid with one of the six methods above, because bank accounts aren't offered on the payment page for now. Its amount must therefore be one that at least one of them accepts: 500 to 50,000 PKR.

### Withdrawals (payout)

A withdrawal's limit is set by the provider of the customer's wallet.

| Provider | `walletType` | Minimum (PKR) | Maximum (PKR) |
|----------|--------------|---------------|---------------|
| Easypaisa | `Easypaisa`, `EasypaisaTillID` | 1,000 | 50,000 |
| JazzCash | `JazzCash` | 500 | 50,000 |

A `BankAccount` withdrawal has no amount limit.

### When an amount is outside the limit

Creating the order is refused with `422` and the code `order.amountOutOfRange`. No order is created, so you can correct the request and reuse the same `Idempotency-Key`.

```json
{
  "error": "JazzCash deposits must be between 1,000 and 50,000 PKR. The requested amount is 500 PKR.",
  "code": "order.amountOutOfRange"
}
```

- Check the amount against the tables above before you create an order, and branch on `code` when the API refuses one. The `error` text states the limit in force and the amount you sent.
- A deposit below 1,000 PKR can still be collected on a Till ID or QR method, whose minimum is 500 PKR.
- The message of a withdrawal names the provider, for example `Easypaisa withdrawals must be between 1,000 and 50,000 PKR. The requested amount is 999 PKR.`
- C2C can change these limits. An order that was already accepted isn't affected: it keeps its payment method, and a retry with its `Idempotency-Key` returns the original order.

On the [hosted payment page](/guides/hosted-payment-page) a customer who chooses the method themselves is only offered the methods whose limit covers the order amount. The method you requested when you created the order always stays available to them.

## Withdrawals

A withdrawal's `walletType` must be one of `JazzCash`, `Easypaisa`, `EasypaisaTillID` or `BankAccount`, and its amount must be within the [limit of the customer's provider](#withdrawals-payout).

`JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR` and `EasypaisaQR` are deposit-only. A withdrawal on one of them is rejected with `400`:

```json
{
  "error": "JazzCashQR is a deposit-only payment method. A withdrawal's walletType must be one of: JazzCash, Easypaisa, EasypaisaTillID, BankAccount."
}
```

## Enabling methods

C2C switches each method on or off **per merchant**, separately for deposits and withdrawals. Creating an order on a method that is disabled for your account returns `422`, for example `JazzCashQR deposits are disabled for this merchant.` A disabled method isn't offered on the payment page either.

If JazzCash deposits were switched off for your account before the new methods were added, `JazzCashTillID` and `JazzCashQR` deposits start switched off too. The same applies to Easypaisa and `EasypaisaQR`. Ask C2C to enable the methods you want to use.

## Fees

Your fee rules are configured by C2C per order type and method. A new method that has no rule of its own is priced by its provider's rule:

- A rule for `JazzCash` also prices `JazzCashTillID` and `JazzCashQR` deposits.
- A rule for `Easypaisa` also prices `EasypaisaQR` deposits.
- `EasypaisaTillID` keeps its own rules. It isn't priced by the `Easypaisa` rule.

Among your account's rules, one for the exact method takes precedence over the provider's rule. The fee is fixed when the order is accepted and re-quoted only if the customer pays with a different method than you requested.
