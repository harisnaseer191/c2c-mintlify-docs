## Overview

Every [deposit order](/api-reference/endpoints/create-deposit) comes with a **hosted payment page**. You don't build any payment UI: redirect your customer to the `paymentPageUrl` from the create-order response, and C2C walks them through the payment in three steps: they enter the wallet account they pay from, C2C assigns a member, and the page shows where to pay.

No member is assigned when the order is created. A member, and the account the customer pays into, is assigned only after the customer has submitted the account they pay **from**. A customer who opens the link and leaves never occupies a member.

<Warning>
  **Changed behaviour.** The customer no longer enters a transaction ID (TID) or IBAN digits, and no longer taps **I've Completed Payment**. They give the wallet account they pay from **before** the payment details are shown, and there is nothing to submit after paying: the member confirms the order when the payment arrives from that account.
</Warning>

<Info>
  C2C has no separate "initiate checkout" endpoint. Creating a deposit order **is** creating the checkout session: one call returns both the order and its `paymentPageUrl`.
</Info>

## The payment page URL

```text
https://<payment-page-host>/pay?order_id=788fcae0-...&expires=1791462080&signature=Xk3...&wallet=jazzcash
```

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Pre-signed | C2C signs `order_id` and `expires`. Any modification makes the link invalid. |
| Lifetime | **15 minutes** from order creation (`expiresAt` in the response) |
| Wallet | `wallet` is the [payment method](/guides/payment-methods) (`walletType`) you requested: `jazzcash`, `jazzcashtillid`, `jazzcashqr`, `easypaisa`, `tillid` or `easypaisaqr`. The page opens directly on that method. A `BankAccount` deposit carries `wallet=bank`; bank accounts aren't offered on the page for now, so that link opens on the list of available methods instead. `wallet` isn't part of the signature. |
| Audience | Only the paying customer. Anyone with the link can see the payment instructions for that order. |
| Withdrawals | A URL is returned for withdrawals too. The page never asks the customer to pay: it collects their receiving account when you didn't send one, and shows the payout's progress. See [Withdrawals](#withdrawals). |

<Warning>
  Redirect the customer as soon as you receive the URL. Don't cache it, email it hours later, or reuse it for another order. If the link expires before the payment is confirmed, the order ends `Expired` and you receive an `order.failed` webhook. Create a **new** order with a new `Idempotency-Key`; you can reuse the same `merchantOrderRef`.
</Warning>

## What your customer sees

The page has the same three steps for all six [payment methods](/guides/payment-methods): JazzCash and Easypaisa, each by account number, Till ID or QR code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the account they pay from">
    The page shows your merchant name, the amount and the C2C order reference, and opens directly on the `walletType` you requested (for example JazzCash QR).

    The customer enters the **wallet account number they will pay from**: their JazzCash account for the JazzCash methods, their Easypaisa account for the Easypaisa methods. It is 11 digits starting with `03`, for example `03001234567`. The **Submit** button stays disabled until the number is valid.

    The browser remembers the number, separately for JazzCash and Easypaisa, and pre-fills it on the customer's next payment. It is sent to C2C only when the customer submits it.

    Until the customer submits, the order stays `Queued` and no member is assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A collection account is assigned">
    Only now does C2C assign a member and that member's receiving account, of exactly the method being paid. If a member is free this takes a moment and the customer goes straight to the payment details.

    If no member is free, the page shows **Assigning Collection Account** with a **10-second countdown** and keeps looking; the order is `Awaiting` meanwhile. If nobody becomes available within those 10 seconds, the order **fails** (`CancelledTimeout`) and the page shows **"Failed to assign the order to a member. Please check with support."**

    The customer is never told why no member was available. You receive an `order.failed` webhook with the same text as its `reason`. To let the customer try again, create a new order: see [Retrying after a failed assignment](/guides/deposit-flow#retrying-after-a-failed-assignment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The page shows where to pay, and the order moves to `AwaitingCustomerPayment`. What it shows depends on the method: see [Account number and Till ID methods](#account-number-and-till-id-methods) and [QR methods](#qr-methods). The account holder's name is never shown.

    The customer sends exactly the order amount from the account they entered, using their own wallet app. **There is nothing to submit afterwards**: no transaction ID, no IBAN digits and no "I've completed payment" button.

    The member who received the money confirms the order when the payment arrives from the account the customer gave. The page then shows the confirmation by itself and, if you set `successUrl`, returns the customer to your site. If the customer reloads or reopens the link meanwhile, the page shows the same payment details again.

    If the payment isn't confirmed before the link expires (15 minutes after the order was created), the order becomes `Expired`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The account the customer enters is their claim, not proof. The order completes only when the member confirms that the money arrived. If the customer pays with a different method than you requested, your fee is re-quoted for the method used.
</Note>

### Account number and Till ID methods

`JazzCash`, `JazzCashTillID`, `Easypaisa` and `EasypaisaTillID`.

The page shows:

- **Only support JazzCash** or **Only support Easypaisa**: the provider the payment must be made with.
- The receiving account number, or the **Account Till ID**, with a **Copy** button.
- The amount to pay.
- A tip: pay the exact amount, and always use the latest account number or Till ID shown for this order.
- The route through the wallet app (below).
- **Within 1 minute, the money will be credited automatically.**

| Method | Route shown |
|--------|-------------|
| `JazzCash` | JazzCash App → Send Money → Enter account number → Finish payment |
| `JazzCashTillID` | JazzCash App → QR Payment → Enter Till ID → Finish payment |
| `Easypaisa` | Easypaisa App → Send Money → Enter account number → Finish payment |
| `EasypaisaTillID` | Easypaisa App → Pay → Till Number → Finish payment |

The Till ID methods (`JazzCashTillID`, `EasypaisaTillID`) also have a **How to Pay?** link that opens a guidance picture.

### QR methods

`JazzCashQR` and `EasypaisaQR`.

The page shows:

- The member's **QR code** to scan.
- **Save QR**, to save the code as an image.
- An **Open JazzCash App** or **Open Easypaisa App** button, on phones only. It opens the wallet app's page in the phone's app store (Google Play or the App Store), where **Open** starts the installed app. A computer has no wallet app, so the button is not shown there.
- **How to pay?**, which opens a guidance picture.
- **Within 1 minute, money will reach your balance.**

The customer scans the code in their JazzCash or Easypaisa app, or loads the saved image there, and pays exactly the order amount.

### Changing the payment method

The page opens directly on the `walletType` you requested and shows no method list. Only a link that names no method starts on the list of methods enabled for your account; a customer who picked one there can go back with **Pay another way** until they submit their account. The account they are asked for follows the method: a JazzCash account for a JazzCash method, an Easypaisa account for an Easypaisa method. Once an account number, Till ID or QR code has been shown, the order stays on that method and that account.

## Withdrawals

For a [withdrawal](/api-reference/endpoints/create-withdrawal) the page works the other way round: the customer **receives** money, so it never shows an account to pay and never asks which account they pay from.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the receiving account">
    Only when you created the withdrawal **without** `customerAccountNumber`. The page is titled **Receive Your Withdrawal** and shows your merchant name, the amount and the order reference. The customer enters:

    - **Wallet Account Number** — checked against the order's `walletType` (JazzCash / Easypaisa: an 11-digit mobile account number such as `03001234567`; `+92…` is accepted and normalised)
    - **Account Holder Name**

    The wallet provider is the order's `walletType` and the amount is the order's `amount`; the customer can change neither. They must submit before the link expires (15 minutes), otherwise the order ends `CancelledTimeout`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing">
    After submitting — or immediately, when you sent the account yourself — the page shows that the withdrawal is being processed, with the wallet, the account (all but the last 4 digits hidden) and the account holder name. It updates by itself. Reloading the page returns here; the submitted account cannot be edited, and submitting the same details again has no effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Outcome">
    When the member has sent the payout the page shows **Withdrawal sent**. If the withdrawal is rejected, cancelled or expires it shows that no payment was sent. If you set `successUrl` / `failureUrl`, the customer is then returned to your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An account entered on the page is exactly as authoritative as one you send in `customerAccountNumber`: the member pays that account. Send the customer to the page only from a session you trust to be theirs.
</Note>

## Returning the customer to your site

Set `successUrl` and `failureUrl` when you create the [deposit](/api-reference/endpoints/create-deposit) or [withdrawal](/api-reference/endpoints/create-withdrawal). When the order reaches a final status while the customer still has the page open, the page shows the result, then sends the customer back to you after **5 seconds**. A "Return now" link lets them go back straight away.

| Final status | Customer is sent to |
|--------------|---------------------|
| `Completed` (or legacy `Confirmed`) | `successUrl` |
| `Rejected`, `Expired`, `Cancelled`, `CancelledAuto`, `CancelledTimeout` | `failureUrl` |

C2C appends the outcome to the URL, keeping your existing query parameters:

```text
https://merchant.example.com/payment/done?c2c_order_id=788fcae0-5214-403c-baa2-f318c0eb1b5b&c2c_order_reference=ORD-20261008120620-3f6b&c2c_status=Completed
```

<Warning>
  These parameters come through the customer's browser and can be edited. Use `c2c_order_id` only to know **which** order to show. Before fulfilling, confirm the status from your backend with a [webhook](/guides/webhooks) or a [status query](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status).
</Warning>

Both URLs must be absolute `http://` or `https://` URLs. Any other scheme, such as `javascript:`, is rejected with `400` when the order is created.

The redirect only happens if the customer keeps the page open until the order is final. A member can take a while to confirm, so also give customers a way back of their own: for example a "Check my order" page on your site, or open `paymentPageUrl` in a new tab while your page polls your backend.

## Integration example

```javascript Node.js (Express)
app.post('/checkout/c2c', async (req, res) => {
  const cart = await loadCart(req.session.cartId);
  const idempotencyKey = `${cart.id}-c2c-1`;              // persisted with the cart

  const order = await createC2COrder({                     // signs and sends POST /api/merchant/orders
    orderType: 'Deposit',
    amount: cart.total,
    currency: 'PKR',
    walletType: req.body.walletType ?? 'JazzCash',
    merchantOrderRef: cart.id,
    customerReference: req.user.id,
    callbackUrl: 'https://merchant.example.com/webhooks/c2c',
  }, idempotencyKey);

  await saveC2COrderId(cart.id, order.orderId);           // needed for status checks
  res.json({ paymentPageUrl: order.paymentPageUrl, statusPage: `/orders/${cart.id}` });
});
```

## Security notes

- The page and its backend endpoints are operated by C2C. Your API key and secrets are never involved, and the customer never sees them.
- The page never asks for the customer's wallet PIN or OTP. Customers pay from their own wallet app.
- The page never asks for a transaction ID or IBAN digits.
- The account the customer says they pay from is stored with the order for the member who confirms the payment. It isn't returned by the Merchant API or sent in webhooks.
- No member is assigned, and no receiving account is shown, until the customer has submitted the account they pay from.
- On a withdrawal the page never shows a member's account, and shows the customer's own account masked after it was submitted.
- Never build or modify payment page URLs yourself. Always use the `paymentPageUrl` returned by the API.
