## Overview

Creates a **deposit**: your customer pays money into an account held by a C2C member, and the amount, minus fees, is credited to your merchant wallet once the member confirms receipt.

C2C creates the order and returns a `paymentPageUrl`. Redirect your customer to that hosted page. It opens on the [payment method](/guides/payment-methods) you requested in `walletType`: the customer enters the wallet account they will pay from, C2C assigns a member, and the page shows the account number, Till ID, QR code or bank account to pay. The customer sends the money from their wallet app, and the member confirms the order when the payment arrives. See the [Deposit flow](/guides/deposit-flow) for the full lifecycle.

The order is **always accepted** with status `Queued` and stage `Awaiting`. **No member is assigned when the order is created**: a member is assigned only when the customer submits the account they pay from on the payment page, so a customer who opens the link and leaves never occupies a member.

<Warning>
  **Changed behaviour.** A deposit used to be answered with `Queued` (stage `Assigned`) when a member was already assigned, or `Awaiting` when none was available. It is now always `Queued` with stage `Awaiting`. If your code treats `Queued` as "member assigned", or expects stage `Assigned` in the create response, update it. `txnRefDigitsRequired` is deprecated and no longer used.
</Warning>

| | |
|---|---|
| **API key** | Required |
| **Signature** | **Required**, in the body's `signature` field. See the [Signature Generation Guide](/api-reference/signature-guide) |
| **Idempotency-Key** | Required |

<Info>
  Deposits and withdrawals use the same endpoint. Set `orderType` to `"Deposit"` to create a deposit.
</Info>

## Headers

<ParamField header="C2C-API-Key" type="string" required>
  `mk_live_...`, your merchant API key. `Authorization: Bearer mk_live_...` is also accepted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>
  Your unique key for this order attempt, up to 100 characters. Retrying with the same key returns the original order instead of creating a new one. See [Idempotency](/api-reference/idempotency).
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="Content-Type" type="string" required>
  `application/json`
</ParamField>

## Request body

<ParamField body="orderType" type="string" required>
  Must be `"Deposit"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  Amount the customer pays, in `currency`. Must be greater than `0`, have at most 4 decimal places and be less than `100,000,000,000,000`.

  It must also be within the [amount limit](/guides/payment-methods#amount-limits) of the `walletType`, both ends included: `1000` to `50000` for `JazzCash` and `Easypaisa`, `300` to `50000` for `JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR`, `EasypaisaTillID`, `EasypaisaQR` and `BankAccount`. An amount outside the limit is refused with `422` and the code `order.amountOutOfRange`.

  **Example:** `1500`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="walletType" type="string" required>
  The [payment method](/guides/payment-methods) the customer pays with. C2C assigns a member with a receiving account of **exactly this method**, and the payment page opens directly on it.

  One of `JazzCash`, `JazzCashTillID`, `JazzCashQR`, `Easypaisa`, `EasypaisaTillID`, `EasypaisaQR`, `BankAccount`. The earlier name `TillID` is no longer accepted: send `EasypaisaTillID`.

  | Value | The customer pays to |
  |-------|----------------------|
  | `JazzCash` | A JazzCash account number |
  | `JazzCashTillID` | A JazzCash Till ID |
  | `JazzCashQR` | A JazzCash QR code |
  | `Easypaisa` | An Easypaisa account number |
  | `EasypaisaTillID` | An Easypaisa Till ID |
  | `EasypaisaQR` | An Easypaisa QR code |
  | `BankAccount` | A bank account, by bank transfer from their JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet |

  No method falls back to another. `JazzCash` and `Easypaisa` mean the account number only: request `JazzCashTillID` or `EasypaisaTillID` for a Till ID. The order is accepted whether or not an account of the requested method is available right now: a member is looked for when the customer submits the account they pay from. On the payment page the customer can still switch to another method that is enabled for you and whose amount limit covers the order, before they submit their account.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="currency" type="string" default="PKR">
  ISO currency code: `PKR`, `INR` or `BDT`. Defaults to `PKR`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="merchantOrderRef" type="string">
  Your own reference for this order, such as an invoice or cart ID. Up to 200 characters. It is echoed in [webhooks](/guides/webhooks), and you can [query the order by it](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status#query-by-merchantorderref).

  <Note>
    One live order per reference: if an order that is in progress or completed already uses this `merchantOrderRef`, the request is rejected with `409`. A reference whose earlier order failed can be reused. To retry a request, reuse the same `Idempotency-Key` instead. See [`merchantOrderRef` rules](/guides/order-status#merchantorderref-rules).
  </Note>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customerReference" type="string">
  Your identifier for the paying customer, for example a player ID. Up to 200 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="callbackUrl" type="string">
  Absolute `http(s)` URL that receives the `order.completed` / `order.failed` [webhooks](/guides/webhooks) for this order. Up to 500 characters. It must resolve to a public internet address: loopback, private and link-local addresses are refused at delivery time, and redirects aren't followed.

  If omitted, C2C uses the default callback URL set in your **Merchant Portal → Settings**. If neither is set, no webhook is sent and you must poll [order status](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="successUrl" type="string">
  Absolute `http(s)` URL, up to 500 characters. When the order completes, the hosted payment page sends the customer here, with `c2c_order_id`, `c2c_order_reference` and `c2c_status` appended. See [Returning the customer](/guides/hosted-payment-page#returning-the-customer-to-your-site).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="failureUrl" type="string">
  Absolute `http(s)` URL, up to 500 characters. Where the hosted payment page sends the customer when the order is rejected, expires or is cancelled. The same parameters are appended.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="metadata" type="string">
  Free-form string, for example JSON-encoded, stored with the order for reconciliation. Up to 1000 characters. It isn't returned in API responses or webhooks.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txnRefDigitsRequired" type="integer" default="4" deprecated>
  **Deprecated: accepted, but ignored.** It used to set how many trailing digits of the wallet transaction ID the customer entered on the payment page. The page no longer asks for a transaction ID on any method, so the value has no effect.

  The field is still accepted so that existing integrations don't break: `4` to `8`, `4` by default. You can stop sending it. See [What the customer submits](/guides/payment-methods#what-the-customer-submits).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="maxRetryCount" type="integer" default="3" deprecated>
  Deprecated: still accepted (`0` to `10`) so existing integrations don't break, but ignored. It has no effect on deposits, nor any longer on withdrawals (see [Create withdrawal](/api-reference/endpoints/create-withdrawal)).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signature" type="string" required>
  32-character lowercase hex MD5 of the other top-level fields (lowercased keys in ASCII order, `key=value` joined with `&`, `null` and empty values skipped) followed directly by your signing secret. See the [Signature Generation Guide](/api-reference/signature-guide).

  **Example:** `f2eb65603d36507621fdfd64aba78974`
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="properties" defaultOpen>
    <ResponseField name="orderId" type="uuid">
      C2C's unique order ID. **Store it.** You need it for [status queries](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status), and it is the `orderId` in webhooks.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="paymentPageUrl" type="string">
      Hosted payment page for this order. Redirect the customer here. The URL is pre-signed and stops working at `expiresAt`. Don't modify it, and don't expose it to anyone other than the paying customer.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      Initial status: always `Queued` for a new deposit. The order is created, the customer hasn't started on the payment page, and no member is assigned yet. See [Order status](/guides/order-status).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="stage" type="string">
      Lifecycle stage: always `Awaiting` for a new deposit, because no member is assigned yet. See [Lifecycle stages](/guides/order-status#lifecycle-stages).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="merchantOrderRef" type="string | null">
      Your `merchantOrderRef`, or `null` if you didn't send one.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="orderReference" type="string">
      C2C's human-readable reference, for example `ORD-20261008120620-3f6b`. Quote it to support.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="expiresAt" type="string">
      ISO 8601 UTC time when the payment page link expires, **30 minutes** after creation.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error responses

| Status | When | Body |
|--------|------|------|
| `400` | A field fails validation, for example amount ≤ 0, an amount with more than 4 decimal places or too large, an unknown currency, an invalid URL or a value that is too long | `{"error": "..."}`. Multiple messages are joined with `; ` |
| `400` | `Idempotency-Key` missing, malformed JSON, or an unknown `orderType`/`walletType` value | `application/problem+json` with an `errors` object |
| `401` | API key missing, invalid or revoked | Empty body |
| `401` | `signature` missing, malformed, invalid, or replayed under a different `Idempotency-Key` | `{"success": false, "error", "code", "traceId"}` |
| `403` | Merchant account not active, or caller IP not whitelisted | `{"error": "..."}` |
| `413` | Body larger than 64 KB | `{"success": false, "error", "code": "PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE", "traceId"}` |
| `409` | `merchantOrderRef` is already used by an order that is in progress or completed | `{"error": "merchantOrderRef '...' is already used by order ORD-…. ..."}` |
| `422` | The payment method (`walletType`) is disabled for deposits on your account, for example `JazzCash deposits are disabled for this merchant.` or `JazzCashQR deposits are disabled for this merchant.` | `{"error": "..."}` |
| `422` | The amount is outside the [limit of the payment method](/guides/payment-methods#amount-limits) | `{"error": "JazzCash deposits must be between 1,000 and 50,000 PKR. The requested amount is 500 PKR.", "code": "order.amountOutOfRange"}` |
| `422` | The `Idempotency-Key` was already used with different order terms | `{"error": "This Idempotency-Key was already used for a different order request."}` |

See [Errors](/api-reference/errors) for the complete error model.

<Tip>
  If a request was rejected (`400`, `409`, `422` …), you can fix it and reuse the same `Idempotency-Key`, because rejected requests don't consume the key.
</Tip>

## What happens next

1. Redirect the customer to `paymentPageUrl` **immediately**. The link expires after 30 minutes. The order stays `Queued` (stage `Awaiting`) until the customer starts.
2. The customer enters the wallet account number they will pay from: 11 digits starting with `03`. On `BankAccount` they first choose whether that wallet is JazzCash or Easypaisa. Only then does C2C assign a member.
3. If a member is free, the order moves to `AwaitingCustomerPayment` (stage `Assigned`) and the page shows the account number, Till ID, QR code or bank account to pay. If none is free, the order is `Awaiting` while the page shows "Assigning Collection Account" with a 10-second countdown. If nobody becomes available within those 10 seconds, the order fails with `CancelledTimeout` and the `reason` `Failed to assign the order to a member. Please check with support.`
4. The customer pays the exact amount in their wallet app. They submit nothing afterwards: no transaction ID and no payment confirmation.
5. The member confirms the order when the payment arrives from the account the customer gave (`Completed`), or rejects it if the payment did not arrive (`Rejected`). If the payment isn't confirmed before the link expires, the order becomes `Expired`.
6. You receive a [webhook](/guides/webhooks), and you can [query the status](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status) at any time. If you set `successUrl` / `failureUrl`, the payment page sends the customer back to you.

| Status | Stage | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| `Queued` | `Awaiting` | Created. The customer hasn't submitted the account they pay from. No member assigned. |
| `Awaiting` | `Awaiting` | A member is being looked for, for at most about 10 seconds |
| `AwaitingCustomerPayment` | `Assigned` | Member assigned and account shown. Waiting for the payment to arrive and the member to confirm it. |
| `Completed` | `Completed` | Payment confirmed. Your wallet is credited. |
| `CancelledTimeout` | `Failed` | No member could be assigned within the 10 seconds |
| `Expired` | `Failed` | The link expired before the customer started, or before the member confirmed a payment |
| `Rejected` | `Failed` | The member reports that the payment did not arrive |

To let the customer try again after a `CancelledTimeout`, create a new order with a new `Idempotency-Key`. You can reuse the same `merchantOrderRef`, because the earlier order failed.

<Warning>
  Fulfil the purchase only when the order status is `Completed`, never on the creation response.
</Warning>
