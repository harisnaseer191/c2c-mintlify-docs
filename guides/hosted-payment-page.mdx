## Overview

Every [deposit order](/api-reference/endpoints/create-deposit) comes with a **hosted payment page**. You don't build any payment UI: redirect your customer to the `paymentPageUrl` from the create-order response, and C2C walks them through paying the assigned member. This works the same for orders accepted as `Awaiting` (no eligible member at creation): the page assigns a member at checkout.

<Info>
  C2C has no separate "initiate checkout" endpoint. Creating a deposit order **is** creating the checkout session: one call returns both the order and its `paymentPageUrl`.
</Info>

## The payment page URL

```text
https://<payment-page-host>/pay?order_id=788fcae0-...&expires=1791462080&signature=Xk3...
```

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Pre-signed | C2C signs `order_id` and `expires`. Any modification makes the link invalid. |
| Lifetime | **15 minutes** from order creation (`expiresAt` in the response) |
| Audience | Only the paying customer. Anyone with the link can see the payment instructions for that order. |
| Withdrawals | A URL is also returned for withdrawals, but customers don't need to visit it |

<Warning>
  Redirect the customer as soon as you receive the URL. Don't cache it, email it hours later, or reuse it for another order. If the link expires, the order ends `Expired` (or `CancelledTimeout` if no member was ever assigned) and you receive an `order.failed` webhook. Create a **new** order with a new `Idempotency-Key`; you can reuse the same `merchantOrderRef`.
</Warning>

## What your customer sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a wallet">
    The page shows your merchant name, the amount and the C2C order reference. The customer picks a wallet from those enabled for your account (for example JazzCash or Easypaisa). C2C then reserves the receiving account, and the order moves to `AwaitingCustomerPayment`.

    If the order is `Awaiting`, an online member is assigned at this point. If nobody is online yet, the page shows **"Finding an available agent…"** and re-checks automatically every 10 seconds until an account can be shown or the link expires. The customer must not send money until an account is shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The page shows the receiving **account number** and **account holder name**. The customer sends exactly the order amount from their own wallet app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the transfer">
    The customer enters the **last digits of the transaction ID** from their wallet app. The order's `txnRefDigitsRequired` sets how many digits (4 by default). The order moves to `AwaitingMemberConfirmation`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    The page waits while the member verifies the payment, and updates by itself to a success or a cancelled/expired screen. If you set `successUrl` / `failureUrl`, it then sends the customer back to your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the assigned member goes offline before the customer chooses a wallet, C2C can reassign the order to another available member at checkout. This is transparent to you: the `orderId` doesn't change. If the customer pays with a different wallet type than you requested, your fee is re-quoted for that wallet.
</Note>

## Returning the customer to your site

Set `successUrl` and `failureUrl` when you [create the deposit](/api-reference/endpoints/create-deposit). When the order reaches a final status while the customer still has the page open, the page shows the result, then sends the customer back to you after **5 seconds**. A "Return now" link lets them go back straight away.

| Final status | Customer is sent to |
|--------------|---------------------|
| `Completed` (or legacy `Confirmed`) | `successUrl` |
| `Rejected`, `Expired`, `Cancelled`, `CancelledAuto`, `CancelledTimeout` | `failureUrl` |

C2C appends the outcome to the URL, keeping your existing query parameters:

```text
https://merchant.example.com/payment/done?c2c_order_id=788fcae0-5214-403c-baa2-f318c0eb1b5b&c2c_order_reference=ORD-20261008120620-3f6b&c2c_status=Completed
```

<Warning>
  These parameters come through the customer's browser and can be edited. Use `c2c_order_id` only to know **which** order to show. Before fulfilling, confirm the status from your backend with a [webhook](/guides/webhooks) or a [status query](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status).
</Warning>

Both URLs must be absolute `http://` or `https://` URLs. Any other scheme, such as `javascript:`, is rejected with `400` when the order is created.

The redirect only happens if the customer keeps the page open until the order is final. A member can take a while to confirm, so also give customers a way back of their own: for example a "Check my order" page on your site, or open `paymentPageUrl` in a new tab while your page polls your backend.

## Integration example

```javascript Node.js (Express)
app.post('/checkout/c2c', async (req, res) => {
  const cart = await loadCart(req.session.cartId);
  const idempotencyKey = `${cart.id}-c2c-1`;              // persisted with the cart

  const order = await createC2COrder({                     // signs and sends POST /api/merchant/orders
    orderType: 'Deposit',
    amount: cart.total,
    currency: 'PKR',
    walletType: req.body.walletType ?? 'JazzCash',
    merchantOrderRef: cart.id,
    customerReference: req.user.id,
    callbackUrl: 'https://merchant.example.com/webhooks/c2c',
  }, idempotencyKey);

  await saveC2COrderId(cart.id, order.orderId);           // needed for status checks
  res.json({ paymentPageUrl: order.paymentPageUrl, statusPage: `/orders/${cart.id}` });
});
```

## Security notes

- The page and its backend endpoints are operated by C2C. Your API key and secrets are never involved, and the customer never sees them.
- The page never asks for the customer's wallet PIN or OTP. Customers pay from their own wallet app.
- Never build or modify payment page URLs yourself. Always use the `paymentPageUrl` returned by the API.
