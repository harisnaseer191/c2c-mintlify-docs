## Identifiers

| Identifier | Example | Who sets it | Use it for |
|------------|---------|-------------|------------|
| `orderId` | `788fcae0-5214-403c-baa2-f318c0eb1b5b` | C2C | [Status queries](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status) and webhook matching. Always store it. |
| `orderReference` | `ORD-20261008120620-3f6b` | C2C | Human-readable reference shown to the customer and used with support. Also accepted by [status queries](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status). |
| `merchantOrderRef` | `INV-10045` | You (optional) | Your own reference, echoed in webhooks and [queryable](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status#query-by-merchantorderref). One live order per reference (see below). |
| `Idempotency-Key` | `INV-10045` | You (required) | Safe retries of order creation |

<Note>
  [Query order status](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status) accepts C2C's `orderId` or `orderReference`, or your own `merchantOrderRef`, and works with **any** of your API keys, including after a key rotation. If an order-creation call timed out before you got the `orderId`, retry it with the **same** `Idempotency-Key` (the response contains the original `orderId`), or look the order up by its `merchantOrderRef`.
</Note>

### `merchantOrderRef` rules

- It's optional. When you send it, only **one live order** can use a reference. Creating another order with a reference already used by an order that is in progress or completed returns `409`:
  `merchantOrderRef 'INV-10045' is already used by order ORD-…. Reuse the same Idempotency-Key to retry a request, or use a new reference for a new payment.`
- You **can** reuse a reference whose earlier order failed (`Rejected`, `Expired`, `CancelledTimeout`, `Cancelled`, `CancelledAuto`), for example to retry a checkout after a deposit ended `CancelledTimeout` because no member could be assigned. A lookup by reference returns the **latest** order with that reference.

## Lifecycle stages

Every order also reports a canonical **stage** in the create-order response, [status inquiry](/api-reference/endpoints/query-order-status) and [webhooks](/guides/webhooks). Stages are the same for deposits and withdrawals, so you can branch on them instead of on individual statuses.

```text
Awaiting → Assigned → Sent → Approved / Rejected → Completed / Failed
```

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `Awaiting` | Accepted, no member assigned yet. Deposit: the customer hasn't submitted the account they pay from, or a member is being looked for. Withdrawal: waiting for an eligible member, or for the customer to enter their account. |
| `Assigned` | A member has the order. Deposit: the customer has been shown where to pay, and the payment isn't confirmed yet. Withdrawal: the payout hasn't been sent yet. |
| `Sent` | Deposit: the payment arrived. This is recorded when the member confirms it, so a new deposit doesn't rest in this stage. Withdrawal: the member sent the payout. |
| `Approved` / `Rejected` | The member's decision |
| `Completed` | Settled: your merchant wallet was credited (deposit) or debited (withdrawal) |
| `Failed` | Final without settlement |

| Status | Stage |
|--------|-------|
| `Awaiting`, and `Queued` while no member is assigned (every new deposit starts here) | `Awaiting` |
| `AwaitingCustomerPayment`, `AwaitingMemberPayout` (and `Queued` on deposits created before this release, which had a member from creation, and legacy `Assigned`, `Frozen`) | `Assigned` |
| `AwaitingMemberConfirmation` (deposits created before this release) | `Sent` |
| `ManualReview` | `Sent` if the customer had already paid, otherwise `Assigned` |
| `Completed`, `Confirmed` | `Completed` |
| `Rejected`, `Expired`, `CancelledTimeout`, `Cancelled`, `CancelledAuto` | `Failed` |

With today's processing, approval and rejection happen in the same step as completion or failure, so the current stage is normally one of the other five. The `lifecycle` timestamps (`awaitingAt`, `assignedAt`, `sentAt`, `approvedAt`, `rejectedAt`, `completedAt`, `failedAt`) still record each step, or are `null` if it wasn't reached.

A deposit normally goes straight from `Assigned` to `Completed`: the customer submits nothing after paying, so the member's confirmation is the only step. `sentAt`, `approvedAt` and `completedAt` then share one timestamp. The same holds for a withdrawal, where the member's single confirmation covers all three.

<Warning>
  **Changed behaviour for deposits.** `Queued` used to mean that a member was already assigned (stage `Assigned`), and `Awaiting` that none was available. A new deposit is now always created as `Queued` with stage `Awaiting` and **no member**. It is `Awaiting` only for the few seconds in which a member is looked for after the customer submitted their account, and it has a member from `AwaitingCustomerPayment` on. The stages keep their meaning: `Awaiting` is "no member yet", `Assigned` is "a member has the order".
</Warning>

## Statuses

  <Tab title="In progress">
    | Status | Order type | Waiting for |
    |--------|-----------|-------------|
    | `Queued` | Deposit | The customer to open the payment page and submit the account they pay from. No member is assigned yet. See [Payment methods](/guides/payment-methods#what-the-customer-submits). |
    | `Awaiting` | Both | An eligible member. Deposit: only for about 10 seconds after the customer submitted their account, while a member is looked for; it then becomes `AwaitingCustomerPayment` or fails with `CancelledTimeout`. Withdrawal: assigned automatically as soon as a member is available — or, when you created it without `customerAccountNumber`, waiting first for the customer to enter their account on the hosted page. |
    | `AwaitingCustomerPayment` | Deposit | A member is assigned and the page shows where to pay. Waiting for the customer's payment to arrive and the member to confirm it. |
    | `AwaitingMemberConfirmation` | Deposit | Not entered by new deposits. On orders created before this release: the member to verify a payment the customer reported on the page. |
    | `AwaitingMemberPayout` | Withdrawal | Member to send and confirm the payout |
    | `ManualReview` | Both | C2C operations to resolve the order |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Final: success">
    | Status | Meaning |
    |--------|---------|
    | `Completed` | Deposit received and credited / withdrawal paid out and debited |
    | `Confirmed` | Legacy equivalent of `Completed` (older orders) |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Final: not completed">
    | Status | Meaning |
    |--------|---------|
    | `Rejected` | Member rejected the order. Deposit: the member reports that the payment did not arrive. |
    | `Expired` | Deposit checkout link (15 minutes) expired before the customer submitted their account, or before the member confirmed a payment |
    | `CancelledTimeout` | No member could be assigned in time. Deposit: none became available within about 10 seconds of the customer submitting their account. Withdrawal: none within 120 minutes, or the customer didn't enter their account within 15 minutes. The `reason` is `Failed to assign the order to a member. Please check with support.` when no member could be assigned. |
    | `Cancelled` | Cancelled by C2C or the member |
    | `CancelledAuto` | Cancelled automatically by the system |
  </Tab>

Legacy orders can also show `Assigned` or `Frozen`. Treat both as in progress.

## Getting the outcome reliably

Use both mechanisms:

1. **Webhooks** for fast notification of every final outcome: `order.completed` or `order.failed`. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).
2. **Status queries** as the source of truth: confirm a webhook before moving money, follow orders in `ManualReview` (no webhook until they're resolved), and catch deliveries that never reached you.

### Recommended polling schedule

| Phase | Interval |
|-------|----------|
| Customer is on the payment page (first ~15 minutes of a deposit) | every 5–10 s |
| `Awaiting` withdrawal (up to 120 minutes) | every 1–2 min |
| Waiting for member confirmation or payout | every 30–60 s |
| `ManualReview` | every 10–15 min |
| Final status reached | stop |

The Merchant API has no request rate limits, but prefer webhooks to tight polling loops and stop polling once an order is final.

## Handling each outcome

| Outcome | Deposit | Withdrawal |
|---------|---------|------------|
| `Completed` / `Confirmed` | Fulfil the purchase | Mark the payout as paid |
| `Rejected`, `Expired`, `CancelledTimeout`, `Cancelled`, `CancelledAuto` | Don't fulfil. The customer can start a new checkout: a new order with a new `Idempotency-Key`. The same `merchantOrderRef` can be reused, because the earlier order failed. | Payout not made. Create a new withdrawal if appropriate. |
| `ManualReview` | Hold. The customer may already have paid. | Hold. Don't re-pay. |
